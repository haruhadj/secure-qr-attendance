# Student Guide

As a student you land on your **dashboard** (`/student/dashboard`) after logging
in.

## Your QR code (digital ID)

Your dashboard displays your personal **QR code**. This is what your teacher
scans to mark you present. Just open the dashboard and show your screen to the
teacher when attendance is being taken.

- Turn your screen brightness up so the camera can read the code easily.
- Your **student ID number** is also shown as a fallback if a camera scan isn't
  possible.

::: tip If your QR stops working
If your code was ever regenerated by an administrator (for example, after you
reported it lost), the old image will no longer scan. Just reopen your dashboard
to display the current one.
:::

## Attendance history

Your dashboard shows:

- A **timeline** of your recent attendance with each day's status.
- A **calendar / weekly view** so you can see PRESENT / ABSENT / LATE at a glance.

Statuses mean:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PRESENT** | You were recorded as attending. |
| **LATE** | You were marked late by your teacher. |
| **ABSENT** | No attendance was recorded / you were marked absent. |

## Submitting an appeal

If an attendance record is wrong or you were absent for a valid reason, go to
**Appeals** (`/student/appeals`) and submit an appeal.

1. Write a clear **reason** (e.g. "Absent on Nov 15 due to a medical emergency —
   please correct my attendance").
2. Submit. Your teacher (or an admin) will review it.

If your appeal is **approved**, you'll be marked **PRESENT for today**. See
[Appeals Workflow](/features/appeals) for what happens next.

## Changing your password

Your initial password is your **student ID**. Change it after your first login
using the change-password form on your dashboard.

- New passwords must be at least **8 characters**.
- You must enter your **current password** to set a new one.

If you're locked out, contact your school administrator — they can set a
temporary password for you.
